About Liam

Speed, skill & showmanship

Meet Liam Collins, The Stunt Runner. Combining a background in elite athletics with the energy of live stunt performance, Liam brings speed, skill and showmanship to his work.

His story spans championship hurdles, world records, GB bobsleigh and Britain’s Got Talent, alongside life on the road.
